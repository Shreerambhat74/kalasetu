# kalasetu — Voice-First Artisan Storefront

**DEVHOST 2026 | Team ByteForce**  
**Problem Statement:** PS 4.2 — Digital Market Access for Local Artisans  
**College:** A.J. Institute of Engineering & Technology

## About the Project

kalasetu is a proposed platform that helps local artisans in coastal Karnataka showcase handmade products online using photos and voice input in Kannada or Tulu.

## Problem

Many artisans face challenges with online selling because of limited digital skills, dependence on intermediaries, and difficulty explaining the cultural value and pricing of their crafts.

## Key Features

- **Speak-to-List:** Create product listing drafts from a photo and voice description.
- **Craft Story and Price Details:** Show product information, artisan-provided labour details, and craft heritage.
- **Ordering:** Demonstrate ready-stock orders with a mock UPI checkout and allow custom-motif requests.
- **Artisan Review:** Let artisans check and edit AI-generated details before publishing.

## Technology Stack

- **Frontend:** Next.js and Tailwind CSS
- **AI and Voice:** Gemini API and Web Speech API
- **Database and Storage:** Supabase
- **Checkout:** Mock UPI flow
- **Notifications:** Planned SMS/WhatsApp integration

## Workflow

1. The artisan uploads a product photo and records a voice description.
2. AI drafts the product listing and craft story.
3. The artisan reviews and edits the details.
4. Buyers browse products and submit an order or custom request.

## Expected Benefits

- Make online selling easier for local artisans.
- Promote Karnataka's traditional crafts.
- Improve product and pricing transparency.
- Help artisans connect directly with buyers.

**Evaluation metrics:** Listing creation time, AI draft acceptance rate, Kannada voice-capture success rate, and mock order completion rate. Targets will be set and evaluated during prototype testing.

## Future Scope

- Add more regional languages.
- Explore ONDC integration.
- Work with artisan groups and local organizations.
- Introduce QR codes for product discovery.
